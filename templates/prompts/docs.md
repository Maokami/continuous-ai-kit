## Documentation and specification consistency

Prefer contradictions that change what a reader would do: setup commands, supported behavior,
API contracts, stated preconditions, or completion claims. Quote both sides of a contradiction.
An absent mention alone is not evidence of a defect. Search the selected sources for the existing
answer before proposing additions. Keep terminology and the repository's writing conventions.
Use kind `documentation` and risk `manual`. Explain who is affected and the smallest correction,
including the check a maintainer should run. Do not rewrite prose for stylistic preference alone.
External facts need separately collected current evidence; do not invent a literature search.
